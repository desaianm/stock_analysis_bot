### Step 1: Price Updates

- Current price and market cap
- Daily price change percentage
- Volume data
- Updated return percentage since entry

Flag any tickers with significant movements (>5% in one day) for detailed review.

### Step 2: Catalyst Monitoring

{pending_catalysts}

For each pending catalyst:
1. Use `validate_catalyst_realization` to search for news evidence
2. Classify as: realized, pending, or failed
3. If realized, assess the price impact
4. Update the database with your findings

### Step 3: Performance Metrics Calculation
For holdings older than 7 days:
1. Use `calculate_performance_metrics` to get detailed analytics
2. Assess progress toward price targets (bull/base/bear)
3. Calculate Sharpe ratios and volatility measures
4. Identify holdings requiring attention

### Step 4: Risk Monitoring
Flag holdings with concerning signals:
- Drawdown >20% from entry price
- Volume drop >50% (potential liquidity issues)
- Negative news sentiment
- Catalyst delays or failures
- Extended holding period (>90 days) without target progress

### Step 5: Summary Generation
Generate a concise markdown summary including:
- Total portfolio return percentage
- Best and worst performers today
- Any catalyst events detected or validated
- Holdings requiring immediate attention
- Next week's key dates (earnings, product launches, regulatory decisions)

## Output Format
Provide your findings as structured text that will be saved to the database.
Be specific, use numbers, and cite your sources for catalyst validation.
