## Your Validation Task

For **each** predicted catalyst above, perform a thorough validation:

### Step 1: Search for Evidence
Use the `validate_catalyst_realization` tool to:
- Search for official announcements (earnings releases, product launches, regulatory filings)
- Find news articles mentioning the catalyst
- Locate analyst reports discussing the event
- Check for social media/Reddit discussions about catalyst realization

### Step 2: Determine Realization Status
Classify each catalyst as:
- **Realized**: Event occurred as predicted, had material impact on stock
- **Partially Realized**: Event occurred but impact different than expected (explain why)
- **Delayed**: Event hasn't occurred yet but is still plausible within reasonable timeframe
- **Failed**: Event won't occur, expectations were wrong, or catalyst didn't matter

### Step 3: Measure Price Impact (if realized)
For each realized catalyst:
- What was the stock price 1 day before the catalyst news?
- What was the price 1 day after? 1 week after? 1 month after?
- Did price move in the predicted direction?
- Was the magnitude of the move aligned with our expectations?
- Calculate the percentage price change attributable to this catalyst

### Step 4: Document Evidence
For each catalyst, provide:
- **Status**: Realized/Partially/Delayed/Failed
- **Evidence**: List 2-3 specific news headlines or announcements with dates
- **Price Impact**: Percentage change with timeline
- **Validation Notes**: Your assessment in 1-2 sentences
- **News URLs**: At least one source link for verification

### Step 5: Update Database
The system will automatically update the catalyst_tracking table with:
- realization_status
- realization_date (if realized)
- impact_on_price (percentage change)
- validation_notes (your assessment)
- validated_at (current timestamp)

## Output Format

Generate a markdown summary with:

1. **Catalyst Summary Table**
   | Catalyst | Status | Evidence Count | Price Impact |
   |----------|--------|----------------|--------------|

2. **Detailed Findings for Each Catalyst**
   - Catalyst description
   - Realization status with reasoning
   - Supporting evidence (headlines, dates, sources)
   - Price reaction analysis
   - Lessons learned (was prediction accurate? what would improve it?)

3. **Overall Assessment**
   - X out of Y catalysts realized
   - Average price impact of realized catalysts
   - Confidence in our catalyst prediction methodology
   - Recommendations for future catalyst identification

## Important Guidelines

- Be conservative: Only mark as "realized" if there's clear evidence
- Cite sources: Include news URLs and dates for all claims
- Measure impact objectively: Use actual price data, not speculation
- Learn from failures: Failed catalysts teach us as much as successes
- Consider timing: A catalyst delayed by 6 months might still be valuable

Your thorough catalyst validation helps improve our prediction accuracy over time.
