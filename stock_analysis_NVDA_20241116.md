# Comprehensive Investment Report for NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA)

## 1. Executive Summary

### Key Findings and Overall Investment Thesis
NVIDIA Corporation has demonstrated exceptional growth and financial performance, particularly in its financial results for the trailing twelve months of FY2024. The company's strategic position in high-demand sectors such as AI and graphics computing, coupled with its leading market position, provides a robust platform for continued success.

### Investment Recommendation and Price Targets
Based on the strong financial performance, technical trends, and positive market sentiment, we recommend a "Buy" rating for NVIDIA. Considering the current market conditions and positive growth outlook, our price target projection for NVDA stands at $175 in the base case, with a potential upside to $200 in a bullish scenario. In a more conservative bear case, we see the stock holding steady at $150.

### Risk Rating and Investment Horizon
NVIDIA carries a moderate risk rating given its technological focus and market presence. We propose an investment horizon of 12 to 24 months to capture the benefits of its growth trajectory and market innovations.
